BACE OPERATIONAL DEFINITION
A claim is a substantive proposition that the source asserts about an entity, action, state, measurement, intention, classification, absence or relationship. Preserve assertions even when false, unsupported, negative, uncertain, prospective or off-topic. Extraction does not judge their truth or support.

ADMISSION: The source must identify what is being asserted, not merely display a string or number. A company name, year and value without a named attribute/metric do not form a claim. "The table records 96.3" and "the report contains 1.7%" are descriptions of source presentation, not substitutes for the missing proposition. An uncertainty note cannot make them substantive. Exclude such fragments as missing_proposition. This does not exclude a source's actual assertion that it did not disclose a value, lacked data or omitted a metric.

Missing qualifiers differ from a missing predicate. A source assertion "Company A emitted 12 tonnes CO2e" remains a claim with an unknown period. A row "Company A; 2020=12; unnamed_column=4%" lacks the metric and must remain excluded. A row label beside several unlabelled numerical columns does not establish which column is an emissions level, reduction, share, baseline or comparator. Do not infer a column role or percentage denominator from a neighbouring labelled column. Retain separately recoverable, explicitly labelled measurements in the same row.

ATOMICITY: An atomic claim has one independently assessable assertion with all its governing arguments and qualifiers. Separate independent predicates, explicitly identified measurements/years/accounting bases, and clearly distributive objects. Preserve the same subject and object population in every child. Coordinated adjectives or noun modifiers that restrict a population are part of its identity, not permission to create different populations. Do not form a Cartesian product of coordinated categories. A combined target/total, collective relationship, comparison, cause, simultaneous effect or condition is itself a relationship: retain it with its full arguments. Retain explicitly stated underlying facts too; never calculate new values or infer unasserted consequences.

FIDELITY: Each claim must independently retain the applicable entity, modality, negation, period, boundary, unit and material conditions. Resolve a target/plan/process/calculation reference with its full material identity, including known magnitude, scope, baseline and deadline. Do not rely on sibling claims or source_quotes to supply this identity. Bind each action to its grammatical actor; bind each modifier only to the predicates it governs. Report/statement time, planned action time and measurement time are distinct. Use only supplied context. If a reference remains genuinely ambiguous, preserve the original breadth and expression with an uncertainty note, never a guessed reading that the note retracts.

Proposition status is independent of layout. A substantive achievement heading is admissible. Bare titles, taxonomy labels, GRI navigation, processing notes and unrecoverable fragments belong in exclusions. Source_quotes must quote text actually supplied; they locate assertions and context but do not replace context in claim_text.

TASK: REPAIR THE ORIGINAL DRAFT AGAINST THE SOURCE
Return a source-faithful, atomic, self-contained inventory by applying necessary identified edits to the supplied draft. The draft is untrusted. Review admission, coverage, grammatical binding, context and atomicity together; do not defer an error to a later pass. Preserve faithful text. Preserve repeated source occurrences; semantic deduplication is a later operation.

WORK SEQUENCE
1. Read the original source and identify each substantive predicate or named metric before consulting how the draft phrases it. Check every draft claim and every proposed addition against the admission definition. When only a cell value is identifiable, add an exclusion, not a claim about a table/report recording that value. Remove an existing pseudo-claim if necessary.
2. For each admitted assertion, identify its actor, action/relation, object, qualifier scope and context. Resolve the corporate actor and exact target/plan/process identity using permitted context. Preserve named subsidiaries and business units. A report attribution alone does not name the subject of generic greenhouse-gas figures or an operational boundary.
3. Repair source-statement versus action/measurement dates, intended versus achieved status, and broad negative/absence assertions. Propagate governing dates and identity to all dependent children. Do not infer a measurement year from publication year or silently restrict a broad absence to a neighbouring measurement. Flag genuinely competing readings.
4. Split only independently asserted content, retaining the original population and complete context in every child. A word "and" does not by itself justify splitting. When splitting is licensed, check that no condition, causal/simultaneous link or target parameter disappears. Retain explicitly given endpoint measurements alongside an asserted comparison; the comparison itself must remain.
5. Reconstruct the final inventory mentally and compare it with every substantive source clause. Check both omissions and new assertions. Confirm every quoted exclusion span is present in the supplied source. Keep correct existing exclusions unless the source justifies changing them.

ADMISSION EXAMPLES
Source: "Company A; 2018=96.3; unnamed_column_7=1.7%; 2019=136.1." There is no named metric. Correct output has no claims from these values and an exclusion covering the incomplete row. "The extracted table reports a 2018 value of 96.3 for Company A" is forbidden even with unresolved_context=["metric unknown"].
Source: "Total Scope 1; unnamed_column_2=511; unnamed_column_5=11.55%; GHG Emissions (CO2e tonnes) 2015=551; % of Group Total 2015=14.93%." Keep the two explicitly labelled 2015 measurements. Exclude 511 and 11.55% because their column roles are missing. Do not invent a current year or copy the neighbouring percentage denominator.
Source: "No emissions figure was provided in the available evidence." This is an actual absence assertion; retain it. Do not confuse it with a cell whose meaning is missing.

BINDING AND SPLITTING EXAMPLES
Source report 2020: "In 2021 we will be formulating a policy to identify and address our impacts, as well as setting out which reporting criteria and standards we will use."
The company will formulate the policy in 2021, with separate stated purposes of identifying and addressing its impacts. The company will also set out the reporting criteria and set out the reporting standards it will use. The shared "we will be" governs formulating and setting out; do not make setting out a property of the policy. Each child retains the 2020 report attribution and intended 2021 action.
Source: "This will provide immediate, short-term and long-term initiatives and processes." If This lacks an antecedent, preserve "This" and note the missing antecedent. Each of the six explicitly distributed initiative/process-time-horizon assertions retains that uncertainty. Do not invent an initiative or actor.
Source: "We develop treatments for patients with life-threatening and debilitating diseases." Keep the full patient description. Two claims about patients with either disease attribute would expand the population. In contrast, "we record and publish Scope 1 and Scope 2 data" licenses record/publish assertions for each clearly distributed data scope.
Source: "Scope 1 comprises direct energy- and process-related emissions from production and energy generation." Keep the stated category description; do not invent four energy/process by production/generation combinations.
Source: "The guidelines adopted in 2013 require products that are particularly environmentally friendly and energy-efficient within their respective market segments." Separate the two product requirements, but retain the 2013 guidelines, particularly, and the applicable market-segment qualification. A policy child still needs the identifying adoption date.
Source: "A, B, C and D jointly established initiative X." Keep the collective relationship, not four independent establishments. Source: "Product X can increase output by up to 20% while reducing energy use by up to 10%." Preserve each stated capability and the simultaneous relationship, including can and up to.

COMPLETE CONTEXT EXAMPLES
Source: "Company A targets a 40% reduction in combined absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 plant emissions by 2035 against 2020. The Scope 2 component covers market- and location-based emissions."
Each component claim identifies the Scope 2 component of Company A's target to reduce combined absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 plant emissions by 40% by 2035 against 2020. Do not turn it into a standalone 40% Scope 2 target. Its market/location-based component metrics need the same identity.
Source: "Targets aligned with 1.5 degrees were calculated for the combined A and B business using a 2019 baseline and submitted for verification. These targets provide a basis for lower-carbon operations."
Submission, dependent target metrics and basis claims all identify the 1.5-degree-aligned targets for the combined A and B business using a 2019 baseline. Do not guess what an unexplained target percentage means.
Source: "We have two 2030 targets: [fully specified target A] and [fully specified target B]. The initiative confirmed the 2030 targets." Resolve that target set and retain each target's full identity in its confirmation. Do not declare this plural reference unresolvable merely because it covers more than one target. Keep a confirmation of their joint pathway as a relationship if also asserted.
Source: "In 2021 Scope 1 fell 5%. No absolute emissions value or base year was provided in the evidence." Keep both absence assertions broad and note the competing broad versus Scope-1-specific reading; do not silently narrow them.

EDIT OUTPUT CONTRACT
The draft_claim_index provides each immutable claim_id with its exact original claim_text. For every replacement, copy the matching ID and text into replaces/expected_claim_text. Never count positions or invent IDs. Unmentioned entries are preserved exactly. Use additions for missing assertions; an empty replacement deletes addressed claims. Every replacement/addition must independently satisfy this definition and the response schema, with source_quotes and unresolved_context (plus context_resolutions for DC).
exclusion_update=null preserves existing exclusions. A non-null update supplies the complete replacement list and its reason; an empty list clears it. Do not discard unchanged exclusions. Remove an exclusion only when its proposition is actually recovered. Return schema-conforming JSON only.

DC SOURCE ROLES
Use the complete disclosure and its own headings, tables and antecedents. Preserve its unsupported assertions and factual errors; no retrieved evidence or reference answer is supplied. Resolve unambiguous corporate context while keeping explicit business-unit/legal-entity names literal. Metadata cannot create a missing measurement date. context_resolutions is [] unless supplied metadata is actually used; then record exact surface_form, metadata_field and resolved_value. Set no_claim_reason=null if any final claims remain.
