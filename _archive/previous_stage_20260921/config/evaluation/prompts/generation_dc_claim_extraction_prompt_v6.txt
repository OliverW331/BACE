Extract claims directly from the source. Do not judge truth, answer relevance or evidential support. Include unsupported, negative, uncertain, prospective and off-topic statements as written.

DEFINITION
A claim is one substantive proposition asserted by the source: who or what has a particular property, performs an action, reports a measurement, intends something, lacks information, or stands in an explicitly stated relationship. Its entity, modality, negation, conditions, time, scope, unit and referents are part of the proposition.

An atomic claim contains one independently assessable assertion. Different predicates, distributive list members, years, regions or accounting bases normally give different claims. Keep a genuinely combined total/target, comparison, cause, sequence or condition together. Preserve both explicitly asserted events and their explicitly stated relationship. Do not invent consequences or duplicate a conjunction merely because its children were split.

A self-contained claim can be interpreted without another extracted claim. Name the applicable entity from the document context. Resolve an identified plan, target, process, boundary or calculation in each child; retain defining dates, quantities, scopes and conditions. Keep explicit business-unit/legal-entity names literal. Do not expand a name into a parent from metadata. Use unresolved_context only for a genuine ambiguity or missing defining information, and keep claim_text consistent with that uncertainty.

PROCEDURE
1. Read the whole permitted context, then work source clause by source clause. Identify the actor, predicate, arguments and only the qualifiers that grammatically govern that assertion. Do not transfer a modifier from one coordinated action to another.
2. Emit each substantive proposition as a complete sentence, resolving explicit antecedents and unambiguous implicit company context. Preserve material words such as historically, proposed, particularly, expected, at least, approximately and only. A purpose is not proof of its fulfilment.
3. Separate statement/report time, action time, measurement period, baseline and deadline. Resolve a source-relative date from its actual report, never a task-year default. Use clear governing headings and paragraph context; do not mark an unambiguous reference unknown merely because it is not repeated locally.
4. Split independent assertions without changing scope. A component of a combined target is NOT an identical standalone component target. Preserve its membership in the combined target. For table observations, retain one metric/value/basis/period per claim, even if a missing column meaning must be flagged. Keep the source's literal damaged unit in the claim and explain the damage.
5. Preserve genuine uncertainty and the breadth of absence statements. A nearby positive Scope 1/year statement does not automatically narrow 'No absolute emissions value was provided in the evidence'. Retain the original absent object and flag competing metric/time readings. Do not replace an unresolved 'This' with an invented initiative.
6. Record headings, GRI navigation, processing notes and predicate-free fragments in unextracted_spans. An identifiable metric with an unknown year remains a claim; an unnamed number with no metric does not. A company's issue classification is substantive. Recover complete assertions before a truncated ending, including intentions and anticipated announcements.
7. Check every source assertion has representation, every claim is source-faithful, and every independent child retains its own entity, antecedent, modality and governing qualifiers. An uncertainty note cannot repair a definite unsupported claim. Do not calculate, normalize questionable data, merge independent observations or add facts.

EXAMPLES
- 'In the 2020report: In 2021we will draft a policy to identify and address risks, as well as set reporting criteria.' Emit the company's planned 2021drafting, the planned policy's identification purpose, its response purpose, and the COMPANY's planned setting-out action. Retain 2020 as report attribution, 2021 as intended action year; do not say the company first planned this in 2021.
- 'The joint Scope 1/2 target is a 40% reduction by 2030 versus 2020. Its Scope 2 component covers market- and location-based emissions.' Emit separate coverage claims for the Scope 2 COMPONENT OF THE JOINT 40% TARGET. Do not assert an independent 40% Scope 2 target.
- 'Figures and indicators covering Scope 1,Scope 2and selected Scope 3 are recorded and published under X.' Emit four method claims: each subject and predicate separately, each retaining that full coverage and X. Separate coverage claims cannot replace scope inside a method claim.
- 'We will continue to monitor laws and will update policies and conduct training as appropriate.' Do not extend continue from monitoring to updates/training or as appropriate to monitoring. If a modifier's scope is genuinely ambiguous, retain its expression and flag that scope rather than inventing a definite attachment.
- 'A completed an inventory to identify hotspots.' Preserve completion and the identification purpose; do not assert that A actually used it to identify hotspots. 'Historically, A's operational-control boundary covered offices and warehouses' gives two coverage claims, each retaining historically and operational control.
- 'Metric M: 2021 = 8 tonnes; 2022 = 9 tonnes' gives two observations. 'A and B jointly emitted 8 tonnes' gives one joint total. 'Our target has 50% implementation' does not mean emissions fell 50%.

OUTPUT
Every claim has claim_text, exact source_quotes and unresolved_context. Put all necessary context in claim_text, not only a quote or note. Use [] when there is no material unresolved dependency. Quote source passages locating both the assertion and its needed antecedents/headings. Repeated occurrences from different source locations are retained; deduplication is later. Return only the required JSON.

DISCLOSURE RULES
Use the entire disclosure_text, including headings, tables and antecedents. The disclosure's own errors and unsupported assertions remain claims. No retrieved evidence or reference answer is supplied.
Name the applicable company in every corporate claim using unambiguous disclosure context; an implicit actor can be clear without an explicit pronoun. Keep explicit subsidiaries/legal entities distinct. context_metadata may resolve company identity or an explicit year reference; it cannot fill a missing measurement year or expand an already named entity. Preserve explicit source-report attribution, but do not invent one or wrap every claim as 'the disclosure says'.
Return claims, no_claim_reason and unextracted_spans. Set no_claim_reason=null when claims exist. Each claim also has context_resolutions: use [] unless metadata was actually used. A metadata resolution records its exact source surface_form, supplied metadata_field and exact resolved_value. Text-to-text resolution needs no metadata entry.
