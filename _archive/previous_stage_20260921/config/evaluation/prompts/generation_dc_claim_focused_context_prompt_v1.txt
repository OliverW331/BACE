BACE OPERATIONAL DEFINITION
A claim is a substantive proposition that the source asserts about an entity, action, state, measurement, intention, classification, absence or relationship. Preserve assertions even when false, unsupported, negative, uncertain, prospective or off-topic. Extraction does not judge their truth or support.

ADMISSION: The source must identify what is being asserted, not merely display a string or number. A company name, year and value without a named attribute/metric do not form a claim. "The table records 96.3" and "the report contains 1.7%" are descriptions of source presentation, not substitutes for the missing proposition. An uncertainty note cannot make them substantive. Exclude such fragments as missing_proposition. This does not exclude a source's actual assertion that it did not disclose a value, lacked data or omitted a metric.

Missing qualifiers differ from a missing predicate. A source assertion "Company A emitted 12 tonnes CO2e" remains a claim with an unknown period. A row "Company A; 2020=12; unnamed_column=4%" lacks the metric and must remain excluded. A row label beside several unlabelled numerical columns does not establish which column is an emissions level, reduction, share, baseline or comparator. Do not infer a column role or percentage denominator from a neighbouring labelled column. Retain separately recoverable, explicitly labelled measurements in the same row.

ATOMICITY: An atomic claim has one independently assessable assertion with all its governing arguments and qualifiers. Separate independent predicates, explicitly identified measurements/years/accounting bases, and clearly distributive objects. Preserve the same subject and object population in every child. Coordinated adjectives or noun modifiers that restrict a population are part of its identity, not permission to create different populations. Do not form a Cartesian product of coordinated categories. A combined target/total, collective relationship, comparison, cause, simultaneous effect or condition is itself a relationship: retain it with its full arguments. Retain explicitly stated underlying facts too; never calculate new values or infer unasserted consequences.

FIDELITY: Each claim must independently retain the applicable entity, modality, negation, period, boundary, unit and material conditions. Resolve a target/plan/process/calculation reference with its full material identity, including known magnitude, scope, baseline and deadline. Do not rely on sibling claims or source_quotes to supply this identity. Bind each action to its grammatical actor; bind each modifier only to the predicates it governs. Report/statement time, planned action time and measurement time are distinct. Use only supplied context. If a reference remains genuinely ambiguous, preserve the original breadth and expression with an uncertainty note, never a guessed reading that the note retracts.

Proposition status is independent of layout. A substantive achievement heading is admissible. Bare titles, taxonomy labels, GRI navigation, processing notes and unrecoverable fragments belong in exclusions. Source_quotes must quote text actually supplied; they locate assertions and context but do not replace context in claim_text.

TASK: COMPLETE SOURCE BINDING FOR THE ASSIGNED CLAIMS
Only review the exact claim IDs in ASSIGNED CLAIMS. The complete original source and draft remain available as context. Return one replacement for EVERY assigned claim, including an unchanged replacement when already correct. Give a short source-grounded reason. Do not edit any other claim. Do not split, merge, add or delete claims or alter exclusions.

For each assigned claim, independently:
- Find its asserted source clause, grammatical actor, object and governing qualifiers. Resolve pronouns/short references from the entire supplied source, not from the draft's assumptions.
- Write the full substantive identity needed to evaluate this claim alone. A component or percentage share of a target must retain the combined target, entity/plants/value-chain boundary, magnitude, baseline and deadline. The phrase Scope2 plant-emissions target is incomplete when the source defines it as a component of a combinedScope1/2 target. Do not move a component into a standalone target. Repeat context rather than relying on a sibling claim or source_quote.
- A process, inventory, policy or arrangement reference needs its defining scope, applicable entity and governing dates. Keep a Group-wide inventory's direct-operations and material-indirect-impact coverage in its dependent hotspot assertions. Keep a paragraph's explicit2020 planned-governance setting in claims about the planned task force, its chair and purposes. Source publication time alone cannot supply a measurement date.
- Check modifier scope and modality. Do not shift a planned event into an achieved event or place the plan's announcement in its intended action year. Do not attach a condition to an action that the source does not clearly qualify.
- If grammar permits multiple modifier attachments, preserve what all readings assert and record the unresolved qualification rather than asserting a guessed reading. Example: for risks are monitored and addressed through preventive actions, monitoring itself is asserted, while attachment of through preventive actions to monitoring may be ambiguous; a monitoring claim must not silently settle that ambiguity. Keep addressing through preventive actions as asserted.
- Keep negative/absence assertions at their source scope. Do not infer that a broad absence applies only to a neighbouring measurement. Preserve and explicitly flag genuine ambiguity rather than using a guessed narrower scope.

Before returning each replacement, compare it with its source and ask whether a reader would need any sibling to identify the target, population, process, date or boundary. Put needed context in claim_text. The uncertainty note cannot retract a claim that was already strengthened or narrowed. Quotes locate source assertions and antecedents; they are not substitutes for complete claim_text.
