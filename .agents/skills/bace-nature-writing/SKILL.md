# Bace Nature Writing

This is a project-specific English adaptation of [the pinned upstream skill](https://github.com/Yuan1z0825/nature-skills/blob/84880815fb37317b3766bff2c2abba395b8993c3/skills/nature-writing/SKILL.md).
Read [BACE writing rules](../../../paper/WRITING_RULES.md) before use and follow
[the official journal profile](../../../paper/NATURE_SUSTAINABILITY_GUIDELINES.md)
when journal requirements matter. Conversation is Chinese; saved outputs are English.
Keep the frozen experiment and analysis environments unchanged.

## Drafting and initial submission

Read the BACE blueprint and evidence map, then the saved sources needed for the
requested section. Establish the question, main finding, evidence and limitation
before composing prose. Keep each Results paragraph tied to an identifiable
comparison or display item. Put replication details in Methods and noncentral
support in appropriate supplementary material; do not hide evidence that changes
the central interpretation merely to meet a word limit.

Use the Introduction to connect the sustainability problem, verified literature
gap and study question. The Discussion should synthesize what the findings mean,
position them against verified literature, explain limitations and state bounded
implications. Do not convert evaluator outputs into external truth or policy
effectiveness. The abstract and title summarize established results only.

Apply the official Article profile rather than flagship Nature/NMI fragments.
Corpus-derived claim progression, paragraph flow and compression are writing
heuristics, not publisher policy. Keep the planned/completed sample distinction
and validation status visible wherever they affect interpretation.

For an initial-submission package, list required manuscript, cover letter,
statements, display items and applicable reporting materials, with readiness and
missing facts. Use verified authorship, related-manuscript and access information.
Keep author-dependent placeholders explicit. Preparing a package is not submitting
it. Revision correspondence belongs to the response adapter.
