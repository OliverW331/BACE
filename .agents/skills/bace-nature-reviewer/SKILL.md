## Internal pre-submission critique

This is author-side self-assessment, not actual journal peer review or an editorial
decision. Use the supplied manuscript/source packet and official Sustainability
criteria. Evaluate originality, sustainability relevance, broad readership,
technical soundness and clarity. Do not claim journal fit or acceptance as settled.

For each substantive concern give a stable ID, claim location, source location,
severity, explanation and resolution test. Distinguish a confirmed contradiction,
a plausible threat and a missing fact. A major concern is blocking only if it
prevents the current evidence from establishing the central case. Do not invent
concerns to satisfy a quota.

Check arithmetic, aggregation, metric bounds, denominators, input provenance and
prose/table agreement. In BACE, distinguish support from truth, engineering checks
from scientific validity, prepared packets from completed human labels, and native
score comparisons from accuracy. Keep statistical uncertainty and population
coverage explicit.

Default to one clearly labelled internal critique. If the author requests multiple
independent reviews, use actually separate contexts when available, share only the
same immutable source packet, freeze the individual reports, and synthesize after
they finish. Otherwise disclose the shared context; never fabricate blindness,
reviewer identities, consensus or independent acceptance. The upstream mandatory
three-reviewer structure is not a journal requirement.
