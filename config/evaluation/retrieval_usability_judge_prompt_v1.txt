## Classification labels

Return exactly one of:

- usable
- unusable

### usable

Choose `usable` when the evidence card can be reliably understood and used in the form presented without guessing essential information.

The displayed evidence should make its meaning and important relationships sufficiently clear, including where necessary:

- the subject or entity;
- the metric, policy, target, action, or other information element;
- how values relate to labels, headers, categories, or time references;
- the relevant unit or measurement basis;
- the relationship between statements, values, actions, targets, resources, or outcomes.

A card may be short and still be usable when its meaning is clear.

A card may be usable even when its content does not address the specified task.

### unusable

Choose `unusable` when the evidence card cannot be reliably understood or used without reconstructing or guessing essential information.

This includes cases in which it is necessary to:

- guess the subject or information element;
- guess how values correspond to labels, headers, categories, or time references;
- guess a necessary unit or measurement basis;
- reconstruct missing sentence or table context;
- infer relationships that are not visible;
- consult the original PDF, table, or another unavailable source.

## Classification rules

- Judge only the evidence text and source information displayed above.
- Judge whether the presented representation can be interpreted reliably.
- Do not classify clear content as unusable merely because it is unrelated to the specified task.
- Do not classify content as unusable merely because it discusses another topic.
- Do not classify a card as unusable solely because it is short, tabular, or contains a processing warning.
- Do not classify a card as usable merely because it contains terminology related to the specified task.
- Missing nonessential detail does not automatically make a card unusable.
- Missing essential context, relationships, labels, time references, or units makes a card unusable when reliable interpretation requires guessing.
- Do not let evidence type, source prestige, retrieval position, or apparent ranking influence the classification.
- Use only the displayed evidence when deciding the label.

## Output requirement
