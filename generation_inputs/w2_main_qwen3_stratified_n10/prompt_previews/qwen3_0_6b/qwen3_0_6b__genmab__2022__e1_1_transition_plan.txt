Genmab’s climate strategy, progress toward carbon reduction targets, climate-related financial risk, relevant prevention and mitigation measures will be presented to the Board of Directors biannually by Genmab’s CSR Committee. The integration of climate-related financial risks into Genmab’s ERM program will be reviewed and endorsed by Genmab’s Global Compliance and Risk Committee in 2022.
