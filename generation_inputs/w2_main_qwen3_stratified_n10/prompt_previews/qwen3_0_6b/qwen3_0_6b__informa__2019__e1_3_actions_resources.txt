REDUCING INFORMA’S OPERATIONAL IMPACTS Informa seeks to mitigate the physical and environmental impacts of Exhibitions and conferences run by the Global Exhibitions and Knowledge & Networking Divisions. At a Company level, Informa’s most significant environmental impact is the greenhouse gas (“GHG”) emissions arising from its office estate. The Company aims to reduce emissions from its larger offices where it controls the energy bill for the building, setting a target to decrease GHG emissions from its 10 largest offices by 10% compared with 2013. This target was not met, with a reduction of 8.2% achieved between 2013 and 2015. Informa’s total GHG emissions decreased by 8.4%. The Sustainability Team runs an annual programme of events each September that educates employees on environmentally friendly work practices and lifestyles.
