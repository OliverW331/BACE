New environmental targets for climate protection Since we achieved our target for the reduction in specific greenhouse gas emissions ahead of schedule, the executive board adopted new environmental targets in February 2019. Our target of a 50 percent reduction in absolute scope 1 and 2 emissions 3 by 2025 compared with the level in 2008—the first full year after the establishment of Evonik—affirms our commitment to the Paris Agreement on Climate Change. At present, we assume an average reduction in climate-relevant emissions of 3 percent a year. The relatively short period up to 2025 reflects our view that it is not currently possible to predict technological and regulatory developments beyond this date with sufficient certainty. In addition to this, we intend to improve upstream scope 3 emissions by 3 percent a year.
