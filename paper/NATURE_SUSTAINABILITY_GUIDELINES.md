# Nature Sustainability: Article profile

Verified against official live pages on **29 September 2026** (Europe/Zurich).
This is a dated operational profile, not a promise that requirements will remain
unchanged. Re-open the official pages before submission or after an editorial
instruction. No page-specific last-updated date was established; the date above
is the retrieval/check date. Source URLs, final response URLs and SHA-256 hashes
are in `skill-audit/official-sources.json`; local text snapshots are retained there.
Cookie-error query parameters occurred in redirects, but the fetched bodies
contained the substantive guidelines quoted below, not just error pages.

## Article limits and structure

Source: [Content types](https://www.nature.com/natsustain/content), Article.

| Item | Official rule | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Title | "up to 10 words (or 90 characters)" | Preserve the stated OR; keep both counts visible. |
| Abstract | Up to 150 words, unreferenced | No citations; every result needs evidence. |
| Main text | Up to 3,500 words | Excludes abstract, Methods, references and figure legends. |
| Main display items | Up to 6 figures and/or tables | Count figures and tables together. |
| Structure | Introduction without heading; Results; Discussion; Methods | Results, Discussion and Methods use topical subheadings. |
| References | Typically up to 50, "as a guideline" | A recommendation, not an absolute ceiling or minimum. |

Do not substitute Nature, Nature Communications, NMI or Review limits.

## Initial submission

Sources: [Preparing your material](https://www.nature.com/natsustain/submission-guidelines/preparing-your-submission)
and [Initial formatting](https://www.nature.com/natsustain/submission-guidelines/initial-formatting).

- Initial submissions need no special visual formatting if suitable for
  assessment. PDF, Word and TeX/LaTeX are accepted; LaTeX submissions need a
  compiled PDF. Final production requirements are a separate stage.
- Prepare a manuscript including Methods and figures, a cover letter, and
  relevant supplementary information when needed.
- Include co-author names and affiliations in the manuscript unless choosing
  double-anonymized review; then place them in the cover letter. The cover
  letter is not shown to peer reviewers. Do not assume the review option.
- The cover letter explains importance and suitability for the diverse
  sustainability readership, discloses related manuscripts under consideration
  or in press, and records prior discussions with editors if any. Reviewer
  suggestions/exclusions are optional and must not be invented.
- Extended Data are optional and limited to ten display items. SI should contain
  material relevant to understanding and reproducing the paper's conclusions.

## Data, code and reporting

Source: [Reporting standards and availability](https://www.nature.com/natsustain/editorial-policies/reporting-standards).

- Original research requires a Data availability statement covering the minimum
  dataset needed to interpret, verify and extend the results. Identify primary
  and reused datasets, access conditions and verified persistent identifiers.
- Describe third-party restrictions precisely. Do not promise open release of
  licensed ESG inputs or report PDFs without checking rights. Make necessary
  review access possible under applicable agreements; discuss restrictions with
  the editor at submission rather than inventing exceptions.
- For central custom code, include a separate **Code availability** section
  after Data availability and before references. Central code must be available
  to editors/reviewers upon request. DOI-minting repository release is a stated
  best practice; a repository or DOI must exist before claiming deposit.
- Relevant life-science, behavioural/social-science and ecology/environmental
  studies need a reporting summary for assessment. BACE should check the
  applicable social/environmental/computational sections at submission; do not
  manufacture randomization, blinding, ethics approval or preregistration.

## Acceptance and production stage

Source: [AIP and formatting](https://www.nature.com/natsustain/submission-guidelines/aip-and-formatting).

- Final submissions use editable Word or TeX/LaTeX, not PDF alone. For LaTeX,
  use a standard class and one text file; remove personal macros and embed the
  reference list from the `.bbl` in the final submission copy. Preserve working
  sources. A special custom template is not required at initial submission.
- References are numeric and sequential. Article reference entries include
  titles. Use the detailed production ordering on the official page.
- Figures are cited in order, prepared at a minimum of 300 dpi and maximum
  width of 180 mm, with 5–7 pt sans-serif labels and editable labels/bars.
  Preserve vector formats where appropriate; do not mistake dpi for a vector
  quality measure.
- Legends define centre values, error bars and their computation, sample size,
  and applicable tests/P values. This does not require inventing tests for a
  descriptive figure. Verify any article-specific legend word limit; no numeric
  legend cap is established by this profile.
- Figure source data are encouraged; statistics source data should be supplied
  as Excel files linked to the relevant figure. Recheck production instructions
  for the actual display items.

## AI assistance and responsibility

Sources: [Nature Portfolio AI policy](https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/ai)
and the journal's preparing-your-material page above.

The current Portfolio page uses a risk-based framework. Assistive use is
permitted; evaluative/interpretive use requires human oversight, verification
and disclosure. Human accountability cannot be delegated. The journal page
states that LLM use should be documented in Methods (or a suitable alternative)
and that LLMs do not meet authorship criteria. Record actual material use and
author oversight; do not rely on older blanket "copy-editing never needs
disclosure" summaries. Internal manuscript self-critique does not replace an
independent expert's peer-review responsibility. Protect unpublished materials.

## Upstream rules deliberately superseded

Generic Nature-family word counts, flagship/NMI formatting fragments,
speech-processing journal conventions, mandatory third-party self-citation,
automatic three-reviewer claims, and fixed model/tool requirements are not
Nature Sustainability author instructions. The installed adapters separate
writing heuristics from the official rules above.
