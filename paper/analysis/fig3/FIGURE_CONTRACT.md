## Scientific question and claim

**Question:** Which diagnoses does BACE assign to unsupported claims, and what
does source inspection permit us to infer from those diagnoses?

**Claim:** Factual boundary distortion and evidence conflation account for 73.5%
of the observed automated diagnoses, but the temporal illustration and the
source-extraction counterexample show why those counts are not independently
confirmed error frequencies.
