# R05: Intesa Sanpaolo (2019, E1-4)

Review status: completed AI internal source review; not human validation.

**Target:** In 2019, Intesa Sanpaolo monitored a greenhouse gas emissions-reduction target covering direct Scope 1 emissions.

**Saved decisions:** BACE = unsupported; ragas = supported.

**Source verdict:** UNSUPPORTED. **Assessment:** BACE better supported.

Under the stated entailment rule, a saved target value and reduction value do not establish that Intesa Sanpaolo performed the activity 'monitored a target'. These are source-dataset indicators, not an account of its monitoring process. RAGAS's reason supports target existence but does not address that added verb. BACE's rejection is better supported under this rule.
