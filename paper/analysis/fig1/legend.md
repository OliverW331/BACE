# Figure 1 legend

**Study design and evidence boundaries.** **a,** The planned matrix comprises 30 companies, five reporting years (2019–2023) and 12 disclosure tasks. Of 1,800 planned cases, 1,798 have completed BACE assessments and both native comparator scores; two incomplete BACE cases are excluded without zero imputation. For target reporting year *t*, PDF reports supply narrative and table evidence from *t*−4 through *t*−1, while extracted metric records cover *t*−4 through *t*. These are source-year filters, not publication-date cutoffs. Retrieval supplies up to ten cards per evidence type, with a maximum of 30. **b,** Solid arrows trace automated processing. Evidence claims (ECs) and disclosure claims (DCs) are constructed by extraction, review and deduplication. Candidate selection uses EC and DC texts. Support assessment receives one DC, its selected candidate ECs, company and reporting year, and assigns direct, inferred or unsupported verdicts. Only unsupported DCs receive one of six ordered diagnoses. The dashed amber branch marks prepared human assessment using the same judgment inputs: two reviewer workbooks contain the same 600 sampled DCs, with annotation cells blank in the inspected local copies. Support judgments remain conditional on extraction and candidate selection; neither those judgments nor the prepared review establishes external corporate truth.
