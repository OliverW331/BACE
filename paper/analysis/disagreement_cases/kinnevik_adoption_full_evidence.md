### E1-1 Transition plan for climate change mitigation

Kinnevik adopted its climate transition plan on 11 May 2020. The plan builds on the climate reporting system introduced in 2016 to assess CO₂ emissions generated by activities under Kinnevik’s direct control. Greenhouse gas emissions and resource use are identified as material environmental topics, with the overarching objective of reducing Kinnevik’s climate impact and supporting emissions reductions across the portfolio.

For Kinnevik’s own operations, the transition approach comprises three principal actions: measuring emissions, reducing climate impact and offsetting residual emissions through an internationally recognised project. Kinnevik conducts an annual greenhouse gas emissions estimate by scope and activity and offsets its carbon emissions in full. Business travel, primarily air travel, has been identified as the most significant source of operational emissions, accounting for 95% of emissions in 2019. These emissions were compensated through a reforestation initiative. Kinnevik recognises that its investment focus on Europe and the United States may require continued travel, while considering physical presence important to active ownership and the implementation of its sustainable business development agenda.

The 2020 climate targets covered 100% of total greenhouse gas emissions and 100% of Scope 1 emissions. Expressed relative to base-year emissions, the reported target coverage was 50% for total greenhouse gas emissions, 74% for Scope 1 and 50% for Scope 3. The target related to location-based Scope 2 emissions covered 75%, while the Scope 3 target covered 50%. These metrics form the quantitative basis for monitoring implementation of the transition plan.

As an investment company, Kinnevik also addresses climate mitigation through active ownership. Portfolio-level objectives are to ensure that companies measure CO₂e emissions, implement concrete measures to reduce climate impact and offset emissions through internationally recognised projects. Prior portfolio monitoring showed that 79% of companies had implemented measures to reduce CO₂e emissions. In 2020, 24% of assets were reported as exposed to material transition risk before mitigation actions, and 74% of assets at material transition risk were addressed by climate mitigation actions.

Climate-related work is overseen through Kinnevik’s governance framework, including the Board’s Risk, Compliance & Sustainability Committee and management’s Risk Committee. Portfolio developments supporting the transition included Zalando’s work during 2020 to establish Science Based Targets aligned with the Paris Agreement, and Tele2’s planned measurement of upstream and downstream emissions with the aim of setting Science Based Targets. Tele2 also continued efforts to source clean power across its markets.
