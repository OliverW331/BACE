### E1-3 – Actions and resources in relation to climate policies

In the 2023 reporting year, Banco BPM’s climate actions addressed both the direct environmental impacts of its operations and the indirect impacts associated with financing activities. The Group’s approach is supported by guidelines for managing environmental and energy matters and combating climate change, with responsibilities and activities assigned to the parties involved and management risks mitigated through ongoing monitoring.

For its own operations, the principal decarbonisation levers comprise the procurement of electricity from renewable sources, energy-efficiency initiatives and systematic monitoring of energy consumption. Banco BPM has historically sourced 100% of its electricity consumption from renewable sources. Operational measures also include monitoring the condition and regulatory compliance of properties, reducing material consumption, using recycled materials, separate waste collection and material recovery, and centralised management of paper and toner consumption. ESG criteria are also integrated into supplier selection. In response to the energy crisis, the Group implemented extraordinary energy-efficiency measures and completed an offsetting project covering approximately 8,000 tonnes of CO₂ equivalent in 2022, contributing to progress under the 2021–2024 Strategic Plan.

The Strategic Plan established carbon neutrality during the plan horizon and envisaged a reduction in total net direct and indirect Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions from 17.5 thousand to 7.4 thousand tonnes of CO₂ equivalent. Reported 2023 target metrics include a 55% target relative to total greenhouse gas emissions in the base year, 40% for Scope 3 emissions, 30% for location-based Scope 2 emissions and 100% for market-based Scope 2 emissions. The reported target coverage for total greenhouse gas emissions is 100%.

Actions addressing financed and portfolio-related impacts include mapping the credit portfolio for environmental risks, integrating climate-related and environmental risks into lending and risk assessment, and conducting portfolio transparency exercises, climate stress tests and scenario analyses. Physical-risk assessments and analyses consistent with the EU Taxonomy have been incorporated into credit processes, while climate risk has begun to be integrated into the ICAAP. The Group also assesses transition risk in proprietary portfolios; in 2023, 18.4% of assets were reported as being exposed to material transition risk before climate-mitigation actions.

Banco BPM supports the transition through green financing products and capital-market instruments. By the end of 2022, Green, Social and Sustainability bond issues totalled €2.05 billion, supported by a framework aligned with ICMA principles. Product initiatives have included green mortgages and financing for energy requalification. The Group’s participation in the TCFD further supports climate-risk governance and disclosure. No separate quantified 2023 climate-related CapEx or OpEx allocation is provided in the available reporting evidence.
