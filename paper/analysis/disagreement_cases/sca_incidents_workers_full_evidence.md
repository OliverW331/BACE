### G1-4 – Incidents of corruption or bribery

SCA does not tolerate any form of corruption or bribery. Corruption is understood as the use of a position to obtain an unfair advantage for one’s own benefit or that of another person. This includes providing, offering, receiving or requesting an unfair benefit. All Group activities are to be conducted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Anti-corruption requirements are incorporated into SCA’s Code of Conduct, which is approved by the Board of Directors and reviewed regularly. The Code establishes principles for business ethics and rules governing employee conduct and business relationships. Group-wide anti-corruption instructions supplement the Code and apply to operations in all countries.

SCA’s overarching objective is that all employees comply with the Code of Conduct, supported by targets that all employees receive Code training and that no violations occur. Employees receive training during their introduction and through refresher courses. Business partners are assessed before cooperation begins, including with respect to policies and processes concerning business ethics. Suppliers are expected to comply with SCA’s Supplier Standard, as regulated in supplier agreements. SCA has also set an objective that more than 98% of contract suppliers approve the Supplier Standard.

Implementation is monitored through checks associated with acquisitions, risk assessments of SCA’s own units and suppliers, and on-site audits of units and suppliers assessed as presenting higher risk. Suspected violations may be reported by both internal and external parties through SCA’s whistleblower system. Reports are processed by the Compliance Council.

In 2022, SCA reported **five confirmed incidents of corruption or bribery**. In connection with these incidents, **five own workers were dismissed or subjected to disciplinary measures**. Reported fines for violations of anti-corruption and anti-bribery laws amounted to **USD 51,615.68**. A separate number of convictions for violations of anti-corruption or anti-bribery laws was not available in the 2022 information. For context, no such convictions were reported for 2021.

The reported indicators distinguish between confirmed incidents, employment-related measures taken in relation to own workers, and monetary fines. The five disciplinary or dismissal outcomes therefore describe SCA’s response to the five confirmed incidents and should not be added to the incident total. SCA continues to apply its Code of Conduct, anti-corruption instructions, training, risk-based controls and whistleblower arrangements to prevent, identify and address corruption and bribery.
