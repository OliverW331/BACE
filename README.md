# BACE: Bidirectional Atomic Claim Evaluation

Code and derived data for the article *Evidence-grounded reliability of AI-generated sustainability disclosures* by Muchen Wang and Melissa Chapman (Department of Environmental System Sciences, ETH Zurich). Correspondence: melissa.chapman@usys.ethz.ch.

BACE evaluates how well a generated sustainability disclosure is supported by the evidence supplied to its generator. It extracts atomic evidence claims (ECs) and disclosure claims (DCs), links each DC to the sets of ECs that support it, and diagnoses why unsupported DCs fail. The study applies BACE to 1,800 ESRS-style disclosures (30 European companies × five reporting years × twelve ESRS disclosure tasks) generated by `gpt-5.6-sol`. It compares BACE with the native faithfulness metrics of RAGChecker and RAGAS. BACE completed for 1,798 disclosures, and all reported results use these 1,798.

## Data

The annual and sustainability reports and the structured ESG indicators used as source evidence come from the [Corporate Sustainability Tracker](https://github.com/forsterkerstin/corporate-sustainability-tracker) and are not redistributed. The repository therefore does not include:
- evidence-card text;
- filled prompts;
- source quotes;
- raw model-call records.

Evidence cards are identified by source file, page and chunk identifiers, so they can be reconstructed from the source reports. Full model-call records are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.

The released tables are enough to recompute the per-disclosure support rate and Evidence Claim Coverage Ratio. Rerunning the pipeline itself also needs:
- the source data;
- the prepared evidence;
- Azure OpenAI deployments.

[`BACE/README.md`](BACE/README.md) describes the setup.
